
PRIVACY POLICY FOR MIRROR LOGIC

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Mirror Logic ("the Game", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a single-player
optical puzzle game developed and published by Appware Tech. This Privacy Policy
explains what information the Game handles, how it is used, and the choices you
have. By downloading, installing, or playing Mirror Logic, you agree to the
practices described in this policy.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the Game.


1. SUMMARY (PLAIN LANGUAGE)

- We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name,
  email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information.
- Your game progress, coins, hints, and settings are stored only on your own
  device.
- The Game shows advertisements supplied by third-party advertising networks
  (Google AdMob). These networks may collect device and advertising identifiers
  in order to serve and measure ads.
- You can reset advertising identifiers or opt out of personalised advertising
  from your device settings at any time.
- You can delete everything the Game has stored by uninstalling the Game or by
  clearing its data from your device settings.


2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not collect, request, store, or transmit to our own servers any of the
following:

- Your name, email address, postal address, or telephone number
- Photographs, contacts, calendar entries, SMS messages, or call logs
- Precise GPS location
- Microphone or camera input
- Payment card numbers or bank details

Mirror Logic does not operate its own user accounts, does not run its own
analytics backend, and does not maintain a server-side profile about you.


3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

To let you continue playing where you left off, the Game saves the following
data locally on your device using the device's own storage:

- Level progress, completed levels, star ratings, and best move counts
- In-game currency balances (coins) and hint or booster counts
- Game settings such as sound effects volume, music volume, vibration, and
  other preferences
- An anonymous, randomly generated local save identifier used only to keep your
  save file consistent on your device

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export or
share it, and it is not readable by us. If you uninstall the Game or clear its
storage, this data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us or by
you.


4. ADVERTISING

Mirror Logic is offered free of charge and is supported by advertising. The Game
displays advertisements, which may include banner ads, full-screen
(interstitial) ads, and optional rewarded video ads that you may choose to watch
in exchange for in-game rewards such as coins or hints.

Advertising is delivered through third-party advertising partners, primarily:

- Google AdMob (Google LLC and its affiliates)

To serve, cap, and measure advertisements, and to detect invalid traffic and
fraud, these advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

- Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, or
  Identifier for Advertisers on iOS)
- Device information such as device model, operating system version, language,
  country, screen size, and mobile network or carrier
- IP address, which may be used to derive an approximate (city or region level)
  location
- Ad interaction data such as ad impressions, clicks, video completions, and
  the time spent viewing an ad

This information is collected directly by the advertising partner through the
advertising software development kit embedded in the Game, and its use is
governed by that partner's own privacy policy rather than ours. We do not
receive your advertising identifier or IP address in a form that identifies you
personally; we receive only aggregated, non-identifying reports such as total
impressions and revenue.

Google's privacy policy and information about how Google uses data from apps
that use its services are available at:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google AdMob's own policies are available at:

- https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543


5. YOUR ADVERTISING CHOICES

You are in control of personalised advertising on your device:

- Android: open Settings, then Google, then Ads, where you can delete or reset
  your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation.
- iOS: open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, where you can
  disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track". On iOS the Game will also ask for
  your permission before any tracking for advertising purposes takes place, and
  you may decline.

If you opt out of personalised advertising, you will still see advertisements in
the Game, but they will be selected on a non-personalised basis and will
generally be less relevant to you.

You can also learn more about interest-based advertising and available opt-outs
at http://www.aboutads.info/choices and http://www.youronlinechoices.eu.


6. PERMISSIONS USED BY THE GAME

Mirror Logic requests only the permissions necessary for it to function:

- Internet access and network state: required to load and display
  advertisements. The core puzzle gameplay works offline; only advertising and
  related functionality require a connection.
- Vibration: used, if enabled in the Game's settings, to provide haptic
  feedback during play. You can disable it at any time from the settings screen.
- Advertising identifier: used by our advertising partners as described in
  Section 4.

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts,
photographs, precise location, or files outside of its own private storage.


7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Mirror Logic is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience, and we understand
that children may play it. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children in violation of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
(COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), or any other
applicable law.

Where the Game is distributed to audiences that include children, we configure
our advertising partners to serve only advertisements appropriate for a general
or family audience and to treat requests as child-directed where required, which
disables personalised (interest-based) advertising and limits the data the
advertising partner may collect.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that a child has provided
personal information through the Game, please contact us at
contact@appwaretech.com and we will take reasonable steps to investigate and
delete such information.


8. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (EEA, UK, AND SWITZERLAND)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
Switzerland, the legal bases on which data described in this policy is processed
are:

- Consent: for personalised advertising and any related tracking. Where consent
  is required, a consent request is shown to you before such processing begins,
  and you may withdraw your consent at any time through your device settings or
  by using the privacy options offered inside the Game.
- Legitimate interests: for the display of non-personalised advertisements,
  frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and the prevention of fraud
  and invalid traffic.
- Performance of a contract: for storing your game progress and settings on
  your device so that the Game functions as you expect.


9. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to,
correction of, deletion of, or restriction of the processing of your personal
data, the right to data portability, the right to object to processing, and the
right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Because Mirror Logic stores its data only on your own device and does not
maintain a user account or server-side profile, you can exercise the most
important of these rights directly and immediately:

- To delete all data the Game has stored, uninstall the Game, or clear the
  Game's data from your device's application settings.
- To limit advertising data, follow the steps in Section 5.

For requests relating to data held by our advertising partners, please refer to
that partner's privacy policy, or contact us at contact@appwaretech.com and we
will assist you where we are able to.

Residents of California may additionally note that we do not sell personal
information for money. Certain disclosures of advertising identifiers to
advertising partners may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural
advertising under California law; you may opt out of this at any time using the
device controls described in Section 5.


10. DATA RETENTION

Data stored locally on your device is retained until you delete the Game or
clear its data. Data collected by our advertising partners is retained according
to those partners' own retention policies, which are described in their privacy
policies.


11. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Our advertising partners are global companies and may process data on servers
located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. These
partners rely on legally recognised transfer mechanisms, such as the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect data transferred across

12. SECURITY

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited
information involved in the Game. Because your save data resides only on your
device, its security also depends on the security of your device itself. We
recommend that you keep your device's operating system up to date and protect it
with a screen lock. Please be aware that no method of electronic storage or
transmission is completely secure.
